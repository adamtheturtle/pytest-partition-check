Code of Conduct
===============

Our Pledge
----------

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

Our Standards
-------------

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive
criticism, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy.
Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, and publishing others' private
information are unacceptable.

Enforcement
-----------

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban
contributors whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. Report unacceptable behavior to adamdangoor@gmail.com. Reports will
be reviewed and investigated confidentially.

Attribution
